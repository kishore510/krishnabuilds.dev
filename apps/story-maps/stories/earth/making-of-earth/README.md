# The Making of Earth

Status: built. The Earth through deep time in 15 chapters: a ball of molten rock, the Moon from a collision, the first oceans, the first continents, oxygen fills the air, Rodinia, Snowball Earth, Gondwana, Pangaea and the Great Dying, the Atlantic opens, the asteroid at Chicxulub, India meets Asia, the ice ages, still moving, and how the story was found. 3 people cards (Patterson, Wegener, Tharp).

- `story.json`: the story data. It is the first story on the `earth-deep` canvas and uses the `earth-deep` basemap: each chapter shows one era of the globe.
- Art added for this story: the `volcano`, `snowflake` and `wave` glyphs.
- Source: every card is **Science**, except the last, which is **History**. Each card's last line says how we know.
- Sources and what is uncertain: the age of the Earth (4.54 billion years) is from radiometric dating of meteorites. The giant-impact origin of the Moon and Snowball Earth are leading hypotheses, not settled facts, and the cards say so. When plate tectonics began is disputed. Coastlines from 535 million years ago to today are simplified from PaleoMAP PaleoCoastlines v7.1 (Kocsis and Scotese, CC BY 4.0) and places are reconstructed with its plate model; older eras and the ice-age ice sheets are drawn by hand and are illustrative. Ancient longitude cannot be measured, so the 535-million-year map is recentred. Extinction percentages and the date India met Asia are estimates.
