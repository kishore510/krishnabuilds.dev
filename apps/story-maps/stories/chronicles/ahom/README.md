# The Ahom Kingdom

Status: built. The Ahom kingdom of Assam, from Sukaphaa’s crossing of the Patkai in 1228 and the maidams of Charaideo, through Suhungmung, Sankardeva’s satras, the paik system, Mir Jumla at Garhgaon and Lachit Borphukan at Saraighat, to Rudra Singha’s Rangpur, the Moamoria rebellion, the Burmese invasions, the Treaty of Yandabo in 1826 and Maniram Dewan in 1858. 13 chapters, a Powers layer and 6 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Sources: the Ahom chronicles (buranjis), first in the Tai Ahom language and later in Assamese; Persian histories of the Mughal campaigns, chiefly Shihabuddin Talish’s account of Mir Jumla’s invasion; Company records, among them Captain Welsh’s reports and the Treaty of Yandabo; and modern historians. Traditional or disputed: Sukaphaa’s dates and the size of his following, Sankardeva’s lifespan of 119 years, Lachit Borphukan beheading his uncle, the toll of the Burmese invasions, and the origin of the name Assam. Native names are in Assamese (`as`). Powers areas are loose regions, not borders.
