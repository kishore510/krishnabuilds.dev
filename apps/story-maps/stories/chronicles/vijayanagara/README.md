# The Vijayanagara Empire

Status: built. The empire of the south, 1327 to 1646, and its rediscovery: the Delhi Sultanate's raid on Kampili, the founding on the Tungabhadra (in tradition, in 1336), the Hoysala lands and the grant at Sringeri, the Bahmani rivalry over the Raichur doab, the conquest of Madurai, Abdur Razzaq's visit, the Gajapati invasions and two usurpations, Krishnadevaraya's reign (the horse trade with Portuguese Goa, the Kalinga campaigns, Raichur, Domingo Paes's city, the court poets and the temples), the nayakas, Rama Raya, Talikota and the sack of the capital, the last capitals at Penukonda, Chandragiri and Vellore, the grant of Madras to the English, and the ruins from Mackenzie's survey to World Heritage. 24 chapters, a **Powers** layer that changes chapter by chapter, and 15 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap (the Krishna, Tungabhadra and Kaveri were added for this story) and the shared art library. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Source: almost every chapter is **History** (inscriptions, Persian and Portuguese travellers and chroniclers, court literature); the founding legend is **Tradition**, and the temples chapter is **Archaeology**.
- Native names are in Kannada (`kn`) by default, with Telugu, Tamil, Konkani, Odia, Persian and Portuguese where the place or person belongs to that language.
- Army sizes and death tolls from the chronicles (Firishta, Nunes) are given as they state them and called exaggerated or disputed.
- Hampi and Kampili are a few kilometres apart, so the first chapter uses the Hampi pin. Powers areas are loose regions cut to the basemap's hand-placed coasts; each piece shows only in its own chapters.
