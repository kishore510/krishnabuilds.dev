# The Later Pandyas

Status: built. The far south from about 1216 to the 16th century: the sack of Thanjavur, the golden roofs of Srirangam, the crowning at Nellore, the Tooth Relic, Marco Polo at Kayal, the war of brothers, Malik Kafur, the Madurai Sultanate and the Pandyas of Tenkasi. 9 chapters, 3 people cards and a **Powers** layer that changes lands chapter by chapter.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes of earlier chapters stay on the map (`journey`).
- Source: History throughout.
- Native names are in Tamil (`ta`), and Sinhala (`si`) for the Sri Lankan chapter.
- Sources: Pandya inscriptions (South Indian Inscriptions and Epigraphia Indica), the Culavamsa, Marco Polo's Description of the World, Wassaf's Tazjiyat al-Amsar, Amir Khusrau's Khazain al-Futuh, Barani and Ibn Battuta's Rihla; standard histories by K. A. Nilakanta Sastri, The Pandyan Kingdom (1929) and A History of South India, and N. Sethuraman on Pandya chronology. Disputed: the reign dates of most kings, since several Pandyas often reigned at once; the year of the Tooth Relic's capture; the story of Kulasekhara's murder, which rests mainly on Wassaf; where Kafur's Barmatpuri and the coastal mosque of Amir Khusrau were; and how the Madurai Sultanate ended.
