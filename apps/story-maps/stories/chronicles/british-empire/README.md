# The British Empire

Status: built. How England's first Atlantic voyages grew into the largest empire in history, how it took colonies from Spain, the Dutch and France, and how it broke up, from Cabot's voyage in 1497 to the handover of Hong Kong in 1997. 36 chapters on a world map, a **Powers** layer (the British Empire and its dominions; the Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch and French empires; and, while they matter, Germany, the Belgian Congo, the United States and Japan) and 17 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data (places, chapters, routes, timeline, powers, people). It uses the `world` basemap and the shared art library. Routes show only for the current chapter (`routes: "chapter"`).
- Source: history. Facts follow standard histories of the period. Death tolls and the size of the slave trade are given as the estimates historians use, and labelled as estimates where they are disputed.
- Powers areas are loose regions cut to Natural Earth 1:110m coastlines and countries (public domain), so they follow today's coasts; small holdings are dots. Each piece is shown only in the chapters it belongs to.
- India's part is kept to two chapters (Plassey, Crown rule and 1947); Company, Crown and Freedom tells it in full.
- Jamestown and New York, and Suez and Jerusalem, are close enough that their pins touch; the map note says so.
