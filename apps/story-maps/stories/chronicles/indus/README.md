# The Indus Cities

Status: built. The Indus civilisation, from the first farmers at Mehrgarh (about 7000 BCE) through the early towns, the planned cities of Mohenjo-daro, Harappa, Dholavira, Rakhigarhi and Kalibangan, their seals and unread script, crafts and trade (Lothal, Shortughai and the sea route to Meluhha's partners in the Gulf and Mesopotamia), the decline after about 1900 BCE and the move east and south (Daimabad), to the rediscovery from the 1820s and the rejected invasion theory. 20 chapters, an **Extent** layer that grows and shrinks with the civilisation, and 10 people cards (the archaeologists: no one from the civilisation is known by name).

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap (this story added the Indus and the five Punjab rivers to it) and the shared art library. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Source: chapters rest on **Archaeology** (excavations) or **History** (Mesopotamian texts, and the modern record of the rediscovery).
- Dates are approximate and many are debated; the extent is painted loosely. Rivers are drawn on their courses today, and the Ghaggar-Hakra, now seasonal, is not drawn.
