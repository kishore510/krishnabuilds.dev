# Company, Crown and Freedom

Status: built. How the East India Company came to India as traders in 1600, turned trade into rule, handed India to the British Crown after 1857, and how the long struggle for independence ended on 15 August 1947. 32 chapters, a **Powers** layer (the Mughals, Marathas, Bengal, Mysore, the Sikh kingdom, the Company presidencies, British India, then India and Pakistan) and 24 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data (places, chapters, routes, timeline, powers, people). It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library.
- Source: history. Facts follow standard histories of the period. Death tolls are given as the estimates historians use, and labelled as estimates where they are disputed.
- Lahore and Amritsar, and Surat and Dandi, are drawn a little apart so their pins don't overlap; the story's map note says so.
