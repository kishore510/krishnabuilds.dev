# Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan

Status: built. Mysore under Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan, from about 1720 to 1799: a soldier’s son at Budikote, the siege of Devanahalli and the arsenal at Dindigul, Hyder’s seizure of power, the treasure of Bednur, the Zamorin’s fire and the ride to Madras, Obavva’s pestle, Pollilur and Porto Novo, Hyder’s death and the Treaty of Mangalore, Tipu’s court and envoys to Versailles, the harsher record in Kodagu and on the Kanara coast, the fall of Bangalore, the letters to Sringeri, the hostage princes, Citizen Tipu, the rockets in a well, the storming of Srirangapatna, the Wodeyars restored and Tipu’s Tiger. 23 chapters, a **Powers** layer (Mysore, the Company, the Marathas, Hyderabad, the Nawab of Arcot, Travancore and the kingdoms Hyder took) and 8 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Sources: Company records, memoirs, court histories and dated events are History; the Nagara rocket finds are Archaeology; Onake Obavva is Tradition.
- Native names are in Kannada (`kn`).
- Hyder’s birth year, the Zamorin’s death, the numbers of people deported and converted, the Jacobin club at Srirangapatna, and Tipu’s motives and reputation are debated; the cards say so where it matters. Powers areas are loose regions, not borders.
