# The Kakatiyas

Status: built. The Kakatiyas of Warangal, about 1000 to 1323 CE: vassals who became kings, the Thousand Pillar Temple, Ganapatideva and the port of Motupalli, the Ramappa temple, Rudrama Devi, the two Delhi sieges, the fall of Warangal and the arch that now sits on the Telangana emblem. 8 chapters and 3 people cards, with no **Powers** layer.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Source: the Thousand Pillar Temple and Ramappa chapters are **Archaeology**; the rest is **History**.
- Native names are in Telugu (`te`).
- Sources are temple and stone inscriptions, the temples themselves, Marco Polo's account of the coast, and Persian chronicles of Delhi. Disputed: early dates, the origin of the name, how Ramappa's foundations were made, the Koh-i-Noor's origin, and how Prataparudra II died.
