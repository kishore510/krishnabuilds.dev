# The Maurya Empire

Status: built. The rise and fall of the Maurya Empire, about 345 to 185 BCE: Magadha under the Nandas, Alexander at the Indus, Chandragupta and Chanakya, the peace with Seleucus, Megasthenes at Pataliputra, Bindusara, Ashoka's Kalinga war, his edicts and dhamma, the missions to Sri Lanka, the empire's break-up and the last Maurya, then the rediscovery of Ashoka's inscriptions. 22 chapters, a **Powers** layer (the Mauryas, the Nandas, Alexander's empire, the Seleucids, Kalinga, the southern kingdoms, Anuradhapura, the Greco-Bactrians and the Shungas) and 11 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Source: each chapter is tagged by what it rests on: **Archaeology** (Ashoka's inscriptions, excavations), **History** (Greek and Latin writers, later Indian texts read as history) or **Tradition** (Buddhist, Jain and Sri Lankan accounts written down centuries later). Dates are approximate and many are debated.
- The empire's borders, especially in the Deccan and the north-west, are uncertain; the Powers areas are painted loosely.
