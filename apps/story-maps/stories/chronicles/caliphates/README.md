# The First Caliphates

Status: built. The caliphate from Abu Bakr in Medina and the Rashidun conquests (Yarmouk, Jerusalem, Egypt and Fustat) through Uthman’s Quran, Ali and the first civil war, Karbala, the Umayyads of Damascus and the Dome of the Rock, the widest reach from Spain to Sindh and the siege of Constantinople, to the Abbasid revolution, the Battle of the Zab, the Round City of Baghdad, the House of Wisdom, Samarra and its spiral minaret, the Mongol sack of 1258 and the last caliphs to 1924. 18 chapters, a **Powers** layer that changes chapter by chapter, and 8 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `near-east` basemap and the shared art library, plus one new place glyph, `minaret` (the spiral minaret of Samarra, used for Samarra only). Routes show only for the current chapter. The story sets its own whole-map view (`overview`), from Egypt and the Bosporus to the map’s east edge. Spain and Sindh lie beyond the map’s edges, so the routes of chapter 10 run off the map towards them from hidden waypoints; Siffin and the Mongols’ starting point in the east are hidden waypoints too.
- No catalogue entry of its own: the Rashidun, Umayyad and Abbasid Caliphate entries open the story at chapters 1, 7 and 13.
- Source: chapters rest on **History**: the Arabic histories of Ibn Ishaq (in Ibn Hisham), al-Baladhuri, al-Tabari, al-Ya’qubi and al-Mas’udi, written a century or more after the early events; Byzantine chronicles (Theophanes) for Yarmouk and the siege of 717; the Royal Frankish Annals for Harun’s elephant; the inscriptions of the Dome of the Rock and Umayyad coins; Rashid al-Din and other Persian historians for 1258. Disputed: Sunni and Shia accounts of the succession, Ali’s caliphate and Karbala; the size of armies at Yarmouk and Karbala; how far Uthman’s standard text replaced others; whether the House of Wisdom was an academy or chiefly a palace library; the death toll of 1258; the story that the last Cairo caliph handed his title to the Ottomans.
- Native names are in Arabic script (`nativeLang: "ar"`).
- Powers areas are loose regions cut to the basemap’s Natural Earth 1:50m coastlines (public domain), so they follow today’s coasts; each piece shows only in its own chapters. The caliphate grows from Arabia to Syria, Iraq, Egypt and Iran, reaches North Africa and the Caucasus, loses Egypt and the east and shrinks to Baghdad; where two powers meet, the land is cut from the one the chapter is not about.
