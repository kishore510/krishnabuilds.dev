# The Eastern Gangas

Status: built. The Eastern Ganga kings of Kalinga and Utkala, from about 498 CE to 1435: the first Gangas at Kalinganagara, the Chola bride and Chodaganga's name, Utkala won and Bhubaneswar, the Jagannath temple at Puri, Anangabhima III as Jagannath's deputy at Cuttack, Narasimhadeva I's war with Bengal at Lakhnor, the Sun Temple at Konark, the southern frontier at Simhachalam, Firuz Shah Tughlaq's raid of 1360 and the Gajapati takeover. 10 chapters, a **Powers** layer (the Ganga kingdom growing and shrinking, the Somavamshis, the Cholas, Bengal and the Delhi Sultanate) and 3 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes stay on the map (`journey`).
- Source: every chapter is **History**. The early chapter is one segment of the timeline and chapters 2 to 10 all fall between 1070 and 1435; the grey gap on the timeline marks the centuries between them that the story does not cover.
- Native names are in Odia (`or`); Kalinganagara is in Sanskrit (`sa`), Gangaikonda Cholapuram in Tamil (`ta`), Simhachalam in Telugu (`te`) and Firuz Shah's name in Persian (`fa`).
- The story draws on the Gangas' copper-plate and temple inscriptions, the Puri temple chronicle (Madala Panji, a traditional source), Minhaj-i-Siraj's Tabaqat-i-Nasiri for the war with Bengal, and Shams-i-Siraj Afif for Firuz Shah's raid. Disputed or uncertain: the start of the Ganga era (usually put about 498 CE); whether Chodaganga or Anangabhima III built the Puri temple (inscriptions and the chronicle disagree); the site of Lakhnor; when Konark's tower fell; and the exact year Kapilendra took the throne (1434 or 1435).
- Powers areas are loose regions, not borders.
