# The Pallavas

Status: built. The Pallavas of Kanchipuram, about 275 to 897, and what they left: the first Prakrit copper plates, Vishnugopa in Samudragupta's pillar inscription and the Kadamba founder Mayurasharma; Simhavishnu's revival; Mahendravarman I (the Mandagapattu cave temple, his farce, Pullalur, Xuanzang in Kanchi); Narasimhavarman I (the taking of Vatapi, the Sri Lankan prince Manavamma, Mamallapuram); the return of the Chalukyas; Rajasimha (the Shore Temple, the Kailasanatha, the envoy to the Tang); Nandivarman II and the poet-saints, Tellaru and the Tamil inscription at Takua Pa; Sripurambiyam and the last Pallava king; and the script that travelled to Southeast Asia and what the 2004 tsunami uncovered. 24 chapters, a **Powers** layer that changes chapter by chapter, and 7 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Sources: most chapters are **History**: Pallava copper plates (Prakrit, then Sanskrit and Tamil), the Chalukya Aihole, Gadval and Kailasanatha inscriptions, the Gupta pillar at Prayag, the Kadamba Talagunda inscription, Xuanzang's record, the Culavamsa and Tang court records. The Mamallapuram, Shore Temple, script and Saluvankuppam chapters are **Archaeology**, and the poet-saints chapter is **Tradition**.
- Disputed: the origin of the Pallavas; whether Samudragupta reached Kanchi; who won at Pullalur and Peruvalanallur (both sides claim); which king carved which Mamallapuram monuments; the date of the Lankan expeditions; the reading of the Vaikuntha Perumal panels on Nandivarman II's origin; the identification of the king who sent envoys to the Tang. The Nandikkalambakam curse and Appar's conversion of Mahendravarman are tradition.
- Native names are in Tamil (`ta`) by default, with Sanskrit, Kannada, Sinhala and Chinese where they belong.
- Kanchipuram, Mamallapuram, Tellaru and Mandagapattu are close together but never shown in full in the same chapter, so none is moved. China and Takua Pa are beyond the map's eastern edge, reached by hidden waypoints.
- The story before this one is The Three Crowned Kings; the story after it is The Chola Empire.
