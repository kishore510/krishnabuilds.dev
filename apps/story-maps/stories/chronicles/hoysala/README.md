# The Hoysalas

Status: built. Southern Karnataka from about 1026 to 1343 CE: the tiger at Sosevur, Talakad, the temples of Belur and Halebidu, Ballala II's independence, the Hoysalas in the Kaveri delta, Somanathapura, Malik Kafur at Dorasamudra, Ballala III's last fight and the World Heritage temples. 10 chapters, 3 people cards and a **Powers** layer that changes lands chapter by chapter.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes of earlier chapters stay on the map (`journey`).
- Source: History throughout, except chapter 1, which is Tradition.
- Native names are in Kannada (`kn`), and Tamil (`ta`) for the chapters in the Tamil country.
- Sources: Hoysala inscriptions (Epigraphia Carnatica, ed. B. L. Rice), the temples at Belur, Halebidu and Somanathapura, Amir Khusrau's Khazain al-Futuh, Barani's Tarikh-i Firuz Shahi, Ibn Battuta's Rihla, and the UNESCO nomination of the Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas; standard histories by J. D. M. Derrett, The Hoysalas (1957), and K. A. Nilakanta Sastri, A History of South India. Disputed: the tiger story and the dynasty's claimed Yadava descent; the dates of the first kings; Vishnuvardhana's conversion by Ramanuja; when and how Someshvara died; and whether the founders of Vijayanagara had served Ballala III.
