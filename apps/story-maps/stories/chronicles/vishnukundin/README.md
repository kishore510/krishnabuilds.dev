# The Vishnukundins

Status: built. The Vishnukundins, about 420 to 624 CE: the dynasty in Telangana, Govindavarman's monastery at Indrapalanagara, Madhavavarman II's horse sacrifices, the cave temples on the Krishna, the Chalukya conquest of Pishtapura and what came after. 6 chapters and 3 people cards, with no **Powers** layer.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Source: the monastery grant and the cave temples are **Archaeology**; the rest is **History**.
- Native names are in Telugu (`te`).
- Sources are copper-plate grants and rock-cut monuments. Disputed: dates and order of the early kings, the site of Indrapalanagara, whether the cave temples around Vijayawada are Vishnukundin, and the dates of Pulakeshin II's eastern campaign.
