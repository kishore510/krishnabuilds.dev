# The Marathas

Status: built. The Marathas, from the Varkari saint-poets to the UNESCO listing of 2025: Pandharpur, Shivaji’s birth at Shivneri, the first forts above Pune, Afzal Khan at Pratapgad, Pavan Khind, the raid on Shaista Khan, Surat, Sindhudurg, Purandar and Agra, the coronation at Raigad and the southern campaign; Sambhaji, Rajaram at Gingee, Tarabai and Shahu’s return; Kanhoji Angre, Balaji Vishwanath’s march to Delhi, Baji Rao at Palkhed, Malwa and Bhopal, Vasai, the raids into Bengal and Attock; the third Panipat, Madhavrao, Ahilyabai Holkar and Mahadji Scindia; the Treaty of Bassein, Assaye and the last Peshwa. 30 chapters, a Powers layer and 8 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Sources: Maratha chronicles (bakhars, mostly written down well after the events) and state letters, Persian histories such as Khafi Khan’s, Portuguese, English and Dutch records, and modern historians. Several famous episodes rest on tradition or on one side’s account and are labelled so: the tiger claws at Pratapgad and who struck first, the baskets of sweets at Agra, the wedding party at the Lal Mahal, the handprint at Sindhudurg.
- Native names are in Marathi (`mr`).
- Shivaji’s birth year (1630 or 1627) is disputed. Army sizes and deaths at Panipat and in the Bengal raids are disputed estimates. Powers areas are loose regions, not borders.
