# The Yadavas of Devagiri

Status: built. The western Deccan from about 860 to 1347 CE: the Seunas at Chandor, Bhillama V's fort at Devagiri, Singhana II's court, Chakradhar Swami, Hemadri, Dnyaneshwar at Nevasa, Alauddin's raid, Malik Kafur, the end of the kingdom and Daulatabad. 10 chapters, 4 people cards and a **Powers** layer that changes lands chapter by chapter.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes of earlier chapters stay on the map (`journey`).
- Source: History throughout.
- Native names are in Marathi (`mr`).
- Sources: Yadava inscriptions and copper plates, the Patan inscription of 1207, the Lilacharitra and other Mahanubhava texts, the Dnyaneshwari, Hemadri's Chaturvarga Chintamani, and the Persian histories of Amir Khusrau (Khazain al-Futuh), Ziauddin Barani and Isami; standard histories by A. S. Altekar (in The Early History of the Deccan, ed. G. Yazdani) and Kishori Saran Lal, History of the Khaljis. Disputed: the dynasty's claimed descent from Krishna and the identification of Chandradityapura with Chandor; the reign dates of most kings; the year of Alauddin's raid (1294 or 1296); the end of Chakradhar Swami; Hemadri's part in Hemadpanti temples and the Modi script; and Dnyaneshwar's dates, which rest on tradition.
