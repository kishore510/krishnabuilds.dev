# The Shunga Dynasty

Status: built. The Shunga dynasty, about 185 to 73 BCE: Pushyamitra's coup against the last Maurya, the two horse sacrifices, Agnimitra at Vidisha, the Greek siege of Saketa, Vasumitra and the horse on the Sindhu, Menander at Sagala, the Divyavadana's persecution story, Sanchi and Bharhut, Heliodorus's pillar, the Kanvas, and Bharhut's railing in Calcutta. 12 chapters, a **Powers** layer (the Shunga kingdom, the Kanvas, the Indo-Greek kingdoms and Vidarbha) and 8 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Source: The inscriptions (Ayodhya, Bharhut, Besnagar) and Sanchi's archaeology are **Archaeology**, except where the inscription is read for a ruler's deeds; Patanjali, the Yuga Purana as a near-contemporary witness, Bana and the Puranas' king list read as history are **History** or **Tradition** as each card says; Kalidasa's play and the Divyavadana, written centuries later, are **Tradition**.
- Native names are in Sanskrit (`sa`), with Pali (`pi`) for Milinda, Prakrit (`pra`) for the Bharhut inscription, Hindi (`hi`) for Sanchi and Greek (`grc`) for Heliodorus and Menander.
- Disputed: every Shunga date (from Puranic reign lengths, which vary between Puranas); whether the Greek at Saketa was Demetrius or Menander; which river the Sindhu was; whether Pushyamitra persecuted Buddhists; whether Bhagabhadra was a Shunga; whether the Agnimitra of the coins is Pushyamitra's son. Powers areas are loose regions, not borders.
