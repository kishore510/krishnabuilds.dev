# The Eastern Chalukyas of Vengi

Status: built. The Eastern Chalukyas, about 624 to 1070 CE: Kubja Vishnuvardhana at Pishtapura, Vengi between the Krishna and Godavari, the Rashtrakuta pressure, the first Telugu verses in stone at Addanki, the Chola marriage ties, Nannaya at Rajamahendravaram and the accession of Kulottunga I. 8 chapters and 4 people cards, with no **Powers** layer.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Source: the Rashtrakuta chapter is **Tradition**; Addanki is **Archaeology**; the rest is **History**.
- Native names are in Telugu (`te`).
- Sources are copper-plate grants, stone inscriptions such as Addanki, and later literary tradition about Nannaya. Disputed: reign dates and order of early kings, the extent of Gunaga Vijayaditya's conquests, the traditional "108 battles", and Kulottunga I's claim to the Chola throne.
