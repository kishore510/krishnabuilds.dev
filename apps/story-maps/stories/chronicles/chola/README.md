# The Chola Empire

Status: built. The imperial Cholas, about 848 to 1279, and what they left: Vijayalaya at Thanjavur, Aditya I and the Pallavas, Parantaka I and Madurai, the Uttaramerur election rules, Takkolam, Sembiyan Mahadevi; Rajaraja I (Kandalur Salai, northern Sri Lanka, Vengi and the Chalukyas, the Maldives, the Thanjavur temple, the land survey, the Chudamani Vihara); Rajendra I (all of Sri Lanka, the Ganges, Gangaikonda Cholapuram, the 1025 raid on Srivijaya, embassies to Song China, Koppam); the Chalukya Cholas (Vijayabahu I retakes Sri Lanka, Kulottunga I, the Kalinga war, Sekkizhar and Kamban, Kulottunga III); the Pandya revival and the last Chola king; and the bronzes, merchant guilds and Great Living Chola Temples. 29 chapters, a **Powers** layer that changes chapter by chapter, and 14 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library (the vimana glyph was added for this story). Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Source: almost every chapter is **History** (Chola inscriptions and copper plates, the Culavamsa, Chalukya and Song records); the later poets chapter is **Tradition** because Kamban's date is debated, and the bronzes chapter is **Archaeology**.
- Native names are in Tamil (`ta`) by default, with Sinhala, Kannada, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Odia and Chinese where they belong.
- Royal claims of conquest (the Ganges water, Kulottunga III at Madurai) are given as claims.
- Thanjavur, Gangaikonda Cholapuram and Nagapattinam are nudged apart so their pins do not overlap, and the note says so. Uttaramerur and Takkolam use the Kanchipuram pin; Darasuram and Tribhuvanam are named on Thanjavur and Madurai chapters. Srivijaya, Kedah and China are beyond the map's eastern edge, reached by hidden waypoints.
- The story before this one is The Three Crowned Kings.
