# The Ikshvakus

Status: built. The Andhra Ikshvakus of Vijayapuri, about 225 to 325 CE: the Satavahana succession, Chantamula's horse sacrifice, the royal women who paid for the Nagarjunakonda stupas, a bride from Ujjain, Buddhist teachers sent to far lands, the excavated city of brick and limestone, the end of the line and the valley under the Nagarjuna Sagar reservoir. 8 chapters and 3 people cards, with no **Powers** layer.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Source: the Ikshvaku inscriptions and the excavations at Nagarjunakonda are **Archaeology**; the rest is **History**.
- Native names are in Telugu (`te`).
- Sources are Ikshvaku inscriptions and the excavations at Nagarjunakonda; the dynasty has no chronicle. Disputed: exact reign dates, the order of some kings, the identity of the Saka queen's father, and how the dynasty ended.
