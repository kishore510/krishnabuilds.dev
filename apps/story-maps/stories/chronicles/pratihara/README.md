# The Gurjara-Pratiharas

Status: built. North India from about 725 to 1090 CE: the Pratiharas holding back Arab armies from Sindh, Vatsaraja and the race for Kannauj, Nagabhata II's capture of Kannauj, the zero at Gwalior, the poets of Mahodaya, the Rashtrakuta sack of Kannauj, the Chandelas' break-away, Mahmud of Ghazni and the Gahadavalas. 9 chapters, 3 people cards and a **Powers** layer that changes lands chapter by chapter.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes of earlier chapters stay on the map (`journey`).
- Source: History throughout.
- Native names are in Hindi (`hi`).
- Sources: the Gwalior prashasti of Mihira Bhoja (the dynasty's own genealogy), the Chaturbhuj temple inscription of 876, Rashtrakuta grants (Radhanpur and Sanjan plates), the Khajuraho inscription of 954, the Dubkund inscription, Udyotana's Kuvalayamala, Rajashekhara's works, Arab writers (al-Baladhuri, Sulaiman, al-Masudi) and Ghaznavid histories (al-Utbi); standard histories by R. C. Majumdar (ed.), The Age of Imperial Kanauj, and B. N. Puri. Disputed: the origin of the Gurjara-Pratiharas and the meaning of "Gurjara"; their first capital (Ujjain, Bhinmal or Jalore); the reign dates of most kings; how far Mihira Bhoja's empire reached; and who in fact killed Rajyapala.
