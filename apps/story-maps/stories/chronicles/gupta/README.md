# The Gupta Empire

Status: built. The Gupta Empire, from about 300 to about 550 CE: a divided north and the first Gupta kings, Chandragupta I and the Licchavis, the Gupta era, Samudragupta and Harishena's praise poem on the Prayagraj pillar, the Naga kings of the north, the southern campaign, tribute from the frontiers, Meghavarna's monastery at Bodh Gaya, the horse sacrifice and lyre coins, Ramagupta and Dhruvadevi, Chandragupta II and the Western Kshatrapas, Udayagiri, Prabhavatigupta and the Vakatakas, Faxian, the iron pillar, Kalidasa, Kumaragupta and Nalanda, the Mandsaur silk weavers, Skandagupta and the Hunas, the Sudarshana lake at Junagadh, the Sarnath Buddhas, the Damodarpur plates, Eran under Budhagupta and Toramana, Aryabhata, the Deogarh temple, Goparaja's memorial, Mihirakula, Yashodharman, the last Guptas and the "golden age" debate. 30 chapters, a **Powers** layer (the Guptas changing chapter by chapter, the later Kushans, the Naga kings, the Western Kshatrapas, the Vakatakas, the Pallavas, Sri Lanka, the Hunas, the Aulikaras, the Maitrakas and the Maukharis) and 15 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, with no additions. Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Source: inscriptions, coins, monuments and copper plates are **Archaeology**; the Puranas, Chinese pilgrims (Faxian, Xuanzang), Aryabhata's own dating and the modern rediscovery are **History**; the Devichandraguptam play and the Kalidasa legends are **Tradition**.
- Native names are in Sanskrit (`sa`), in Devanagari, with Chinese (`zh`) for Faxian.
- Reign dates are approximate, the order of the kings after Budhagupta is debated, and so are Ramagupta, Kalidasa's dates and how far Samudragupta's claimed conquests were real. The cards say so where it matters. Powers areas are loose regions, not borders.
