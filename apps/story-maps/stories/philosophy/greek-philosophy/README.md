# Greek Philosophy

Status: built. In Philosophy. How Greek thinkers looked for natural causes and argued about change, atoms, the good life and how we can know anything, from c. 585 BCE to the 13th century, in 14 chapters: Thales and the Milesians, Pythagoras, Heraclitus, Parmenides and Zeno, Democritus, the sophists and Socrates, Plato's Academy, Aristotle, Pyrrho, Epicurus and the Stoics, Rome, Plotinus, the last philosophers of Athens, and Aristotle in Arabic and Latin. 3 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `europe` basemap, shared with the Lives of Socrates, Plato and Aristotle. Routes show only for the current chapter. Kition, Alexandria and the lands to the east (Persia, India, Baghdad) lie off the map, so they are hidden waypoints at its south and east edges.
- No new art: it uses the `greek-temple`, `anchor`, `village`, `boat`, `dome` and `palace` glyphs and the `merchant-ship` and `scroll` traveller icons.
- Source: **History**, every chapter.
- Native names are in Ancient Greek in Latin letters (`grc`); chapter 11 is in Latin (`la`) and chapter 14 and Ibn Rushd in Arabic (`ar`).
- Sources and what is disputed: the early philosophers' books are lost, so the cards follow fragments quoted by later writers (Plato, Aristotle, Simplicius, Sextus Empiricus) and the doxographers, and say so. Thales' eclipse is from Herodotus; its date of 585 BCE is the usual identification. The stories about Pythagoras come from writers centuries later (Porphyry, Iamblichus), and the theorem was known before him. Plato's one-line summary of Heraclitus is from the Cratylus. Leucippus' home is disputed. Pyrrho's meeting with the Indian ascetics comes from Diogenes Laertius. How suddenly the Athenian school ended in 529 is disputed; the journey to Khosrow follows Agathias. The dates of Pythagoras, Democritus and Zeno of Kition are rough; Hypatia's birth year is not known. Socrates, Plato and Aristotle have their own Lives, with fuller sources.
