# The Odyssey

Status: built. The Odyssey of Greek tradition in 25 chapters on the `europe` basemap, from Troy and the wanderings of Odysseus to his return to Ithaca and the palace at Pylos.

- `story.json`: the story data (places, chapters, routes, timeline, people). It uses the `europe` basemap and the shared art library.
- Sources: Homer’s Odyssey for chapters 2 to 23, with ancient identifications of its places from Herodotus, Thucydides, Strabo and Roman writers, and the excavations at Pylos (Blegen, from 1939) and on Ithaca for chapters 24 and 25. Disputed or uncertain: who Homer was and when the poem was composed (usually about 725 to 650 BCE); whether the wanderings follow any real geography at all; the sites of the Lotus-eaters, the Laestrygonians (Formiae or Bonifacio), Aeaea, the entrance to the dead, Thrinacia, Ogygia (Gozo is one claim of many) and Scheria; and whether Homer’s Ithaca is the modern island, Lefkada or Kefalonia’s Paliki.
