# The Iliad

Status: built. The Iliad and the Trojan War of Greek tradition in 25 chapters on the `near-east` basemap, from the judgement of Paris to the funeral of Hector, then the fall of Troy and the digs at Hisarlık and Mycenae.

- `story.json`: the story data (places, chapters, routes, timeline, people). It uses the `near-east` basemap and the shared art library.
- Sources: Homer’s Iliad for chapters 8 to 22; for what happens before and after it, the summaries of the lost Epic Cycle (the Cypria and the Aethiopis), the Odyssey and later Greek and Roman writers. Disputed or uncertain: who Homer was and when the poem was composed (usually about 750 to 650 BCE); whether a Trojan War happened at all, and whether the burned layer Troy VIIa at Hisarlık records it; the Hittite kingdom of Wilusa as Ilios; the sites of Phthia, Thebe under Placus and Chryse; and the date of the fall (Eratosthenes’ 1184 BCE is one ancient reckoning among several).
