# The Mahabharata

Status: built. The Kuru cousins’ story as the epic tells it, in 39 chapters: Vyasa and Ganesha at Mana, Shantanu and Ganga, Bhishma’s vow, the princesses of Kashi, the three princes, Karna set adrift to Champa, the births of the Pandavas and Kauravas, Drona’s tournament and Ekalavya, Drona’s revenge on Drupada (Ahichchhatra), the house of lac at Varanavata, Hidimba and Baka at Ekachakra, Draupadi’s choice at Kampilya, Indraprastha and the Khandava forest, Arjuna in Manipura and Dwaraka, Jarasandha at Girivraja, the royal consecration and Shishupala, the dice game and Draupadi in the hall, the forest exile (Kamyaka, Bhima and Hanuman near Badari, the yaksha’s questions), the year in hiding at Virata’s city, Krishna’s peace mission and Karna’s choice, the eighteen days at Kurukshetra (the Bhagavad Gita, Bhishma’s fall, Abhimanyu, Drona, Karna, Duryodhana, the night raid), the women’s lament, Bhishma’s teachings, the horse sacrifice in Manipura, the end of the Yadavas at Prabhasa, the sea taking Dwaraka, the great journey from Mana, and the critical edition made at Pune. A **Kingdoms** layer (Kuru, Panchala, Matsya, the Yadavas, Magadha, Anga and the kingdoms that join the war) and 16 people cards.

- `story.json`: the story data. It uses the `south-asia` basemap and the shared art library, plus one new glyph (a burning house, for the house of lac). Routes show only for the current chapter.
- Source: **Tradition** throughout, except the last chapter (the critical edition), which is **History**. The text follows the Sanskrit epic, mainly the Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute’s critical edition, and says where well-known episodes (Ganesha as scribe, Krishna answering Draupadi’s prayer, Draupadi’s jibe at Duryodhana) are later additions.
- Places are where tradition puts them. Where several places claim one (Ekachakra), the card names them; where the epic’s place is not identified (the Dvaita forest), the card says so.
- Native names are in Sanskrit (`sa`), in Devanagari.
