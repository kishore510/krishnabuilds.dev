# Missions to the Sun

Status: built. The missions to the Sun in 13 chapters: Helios inside Mercury’s orbit, Ulysses out of the planets’ plane and over the poles, SOHO at L1, lost and found, comets and a ringing Sun, Genesis’s sample and crash landing, Parker’s Venus passes and first touch of the corona, Solar Orbiter at the poles, Aditya-L1 and Parker’s closest pass. 1 people card (Parker).

- `story.json`: the story data. It is on the `cosmic` canvas and uses the `solar-system` sky basemap. The map is a diagram of distance, not a picture of space: left to right is distance from the Sun on a logarithmic scale, and up and down mean nothing, except directly above the Sun, which stands for its poles. The closest passes of Helios and Parker and the Lagrange point L1 would fall off the map or on top of Earth on that scale, so they are drawn off scale, and the `note` says so. Routes show one chapter at a time (`routes: "chapter"`), and “Whole map” frames the Sun to Jupiter (`overview`).
- Art: no new drawings.
- Source: History by default; chapters 3, 6 and 10 are **Science**.
- Sources and what is uncertain: Dates, distances and figures follow NASA (including the National Space Science Data Center and the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory Parker Solar Probe pages), ESA and ISRO. The closest passes and L1 are drawn off scale, as the note says. What is uncertain: Helios’s speed record is often given as about 250,000 kilometres an hour, with small differences between sources; the Kreutz comets are thought, not known, to come from one parent comet; the cause of the Sun’s oxygen difference from Earth found by Genesis is argued; and why the corona is so much hotter than the surface, the question Parker and Solar Orbiter were built to answer, is still open. Solar Orbiter’s later orbit tilts are ESA’s planned figures at the time of writing.
