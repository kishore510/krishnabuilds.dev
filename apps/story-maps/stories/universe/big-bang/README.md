# The Big Bang

Status: built. The history of the universe in 15 chapters: the Big Bang, inflation, the first particles, the first nuclei, first light (the cosmic microwave background), the Dark Ages, the first stars, the first galaxies, the Milky Way, cosmic noon, the expansion speeding up, the Sun and its planets, Earth and the Moon, the universe today, and how the story was found. 3 people cards (Lemaître, Hubble, Penzias and Wilson).

- `story.json`: the story data. It is the first story on the `cosmic` canvas and uses the `cosmos` sky basemap. The map is a diagram of time, not of space: left is earlier, right is later, and the widening shape stands for the expansion. It is not to scale.
- Art added for this story: the `burst`, `swell`, `particles`, `atom`, `light-waves`, `gas-cloud`, `star`, `galaxy`, `sun`, `earth-moon`, `speed-up` and `telescope` glyphs and the `light` traveller icon.
- Source: every card is **Science**, except the last, on the discoveries of 1927 to 1964, which is **History**. Each card's last line says how we know.
- Sources and what is uncertain: figures follow NASA, ESA and the Planck satellite's 2018 results (age 13.8 billion years; about 5 percent ordinary matter and 68 percent dark energy). Inflation is a well-supported theory, not an observation, and the card says so. Nobody has yet seen a first-generation star; the timing of the first stars comes from models. The Moon's origin in a giant impact is the leading explanation, not a settled fact. The count of galaxies is an estimate.
