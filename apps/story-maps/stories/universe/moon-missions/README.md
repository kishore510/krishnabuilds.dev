# Missions to the Moon

Status: built. The missions to the Moon in 23 chapters: Luna 2's impact and Luna 3's far-side photographs, Ranger 7, Luna 9's soft landing, Apollo 11 and 12, Luna 16, Lunokhod 1, Apollo 13 and Fra Mauro, Apollo 15 and 17, Lunokhod 2, Luna 24, ice at the poles, Chang'e 3, 4, 5 and 6, Chandrayaan-3, SLIM, Blue Ghost, Artemis II and the race for the south pole. 4 people cards (Korolev, Armstrong, Schmitt, Koch).

- `story.json`: the story data. It is on the `cosmic` canvas and uses the `moon` sky basemap, whose units are selenographic longitude and latitude, so every place is a real [lon, lat] on the Moon. There are no routes: every chapter has `legs: []`.
- Art added for this story: the `lander` and `rover` glyphs.
- Source: History by default, the way the missions were run and what they found; the chapter on polar ice is **Science**.
- Sources and what is uncertain: landing and impact sites, dates and sample masses follow NASA, the Soviet and Russian space agencies as reported by NASA's National Space Science Data Center, CNSA, ISRO and JAXA, and the IAU Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature for official site names (Statio Tranquillitatis, Tianhe, Tianchuan, Tianjiang, Shiv Shakti). Early impact sites, Luna 2's above all, are estimates. Lunokhod 2's distance is given as 39 to 42 kilometres because orbiter images in 2013 revised the Soviet figure. The amount of polar ice is an estimate; its presence is measured. Chapter 23 gives plans as of November 2026 (Chang'e 7 postponed to 2027; Artemis III now an Earth-orbit test in 2027 and the first Artemis landing planned for 2028); they will change. The map is the `moon` basemap, made from NASA LRO's LROC mare boundaries and the IAU gazetteer. New art for this story: the `lander` and `rover` glyphs.
