# Saturn and Titan

Status: built. The missions to Saturn in 14 chapters: Pioneer 11, Voyager 1 and Titan’s haze, Voyager 2 and the rings, the long road of Cassini–Huygens, Huygens’ landing on Titan, the jets of Enceladus, the seas of Titan, the hexagon, the great white storm, the ocean under Enceladus’s ice, the Grand Finale, Cassini’s last plunge, the age of the rings, and Dragonfly. 3 people cards (Huygens, Cassini, Porco).

- `story.json`: the story data. It is on the `cosmic` canvas with the NEW `saturn-system` sky basemap: Saturn and its main rings to scale with the polar hexagon, the moons from Mimas to Phoebe on a logarithmic distance scale, the E ring, the edge of the magnetosphere and an enlarged Titan globe with its northern seas and dune fields. Routes show one chapter at a time (`routes: "chapter"`).
- Art: no new drawings.
- Source: History by default; chapters 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 13 are **Science**.
- Sources and what is uncertain: Dates, distances and figures follow NASA (the National Space Science Data Center, JPL and mission pages) and ESA. Sources give Huygens’ time on the surface as 72 minutes (NASA) or about 90 (others), so the card says over an hour. The causes of the hexagon’s six sides and of the spokes are theory. The rings’ age is disputed: the Cassini-era case for a few hundred million years against the 2024 argument (Hyodo and colleagues, Nature Geoscience) that they could be as old as Saturn; how fast they are losing material varies between estimates. Enceladus’s ocean is inferred from gravity and spin measurements, not seen. Dragonfly’s July 2028 launch and 2034 arrival are plans at the time of writing: update chapter 14 once it has launched. The map is a diagram: the Titan globe is viewed from 38° north over 210° west longitude, with the Huygens site at 10.6° S 192.3° W, Selk crater at about 7° N 199° W and Ligeia Mare at 79° N 248° W. The basemap was drawn by a script that is not kept in the repo, like jupiter-system.
