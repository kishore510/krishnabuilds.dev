# Asteroids and Comets

Status: built. The spacecraft sent to asteroids and comets in 14 chapters: Giotto at Halley’s Comet, Galileo at Ida and Dactyl, NEAR at Eros, Stardust and Deep Impact, Hayabusa at Itokawa, Dawn at Vesta and Ceres, Rosetta and Philae at Comet 67P, Hayabusa2 at Ryugu, OSIRIS-REx at Bennu, DART at Dimorphos and what comes next. 4 people cards (Shoemaker, Kawaguchi, Gerasimenko, Lauretta).

- `story.json`: the story data. It is on the `cosmic` canvas and uses the `solar-system` sky basemap. The map is a diagram of distance, not a picture of space: left to right is distance from the Sun on a logarithmic scale, and up and down mean nothing (comets are drawn above the planets’ plane, near-Earth asteroids below it, only to keep them apart). Routes show one chapter at a time (`routes: "chapter"`), as every leg starts at Earth.
- Art added for this story: the `comet`, `dwarf-planet` and `rocky-planet` glyphs. The last two are shared with the Pluto and Mercury stories.
- Source: History by default, the way each mission was flown; the cards on Stardust’s dust, Ceres and Rosetta are **Science**. Native names (Hayabusa) are in Japanese.
- Not drawn: a “Main asteroid belt” regions layer. The realm format needs rulers, facts and a role for every region, which a band of orbits cannot honestly fill, so the belt is left to the basemap.
- Sources and what is uncertain: dates, distances, sizes and sample masses follow NASA (including JPL and the National Space Science Data Center), ESA and JAXA mission pages. Sizes of irregular bodies are rounded longest lengths or mean diameters. Positions on the map are each body's distance from the Sun at the visit, rounded, on the solar-system map's logarithmic scale; the up-and-down placing is only to keep bodies apart. What is uncertain: the change in Dimorphos's orbit is NASA's measured value of about 32 minutes (later refined to about 33); whether comets brought much of Earth's water is still argued, and Rosetta's heavy-hydrogen result is one measurement of one comet; Hera's arrival and Lucy's and Psyche's later encounters are planned dates. The `comet`, `dwarf-planet` and `rocky-planet` glyphs are new (the last two are shared with the Pluto and Mercury stories).
