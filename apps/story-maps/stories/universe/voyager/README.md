# The Voyager Probes

Status: built. The two Voyager probes in 15 chapters: the Grand Tour line-up of 1965, the two launches of 1977, the Golden Record, Jupiter, Titan and Saturn, Saturn's rings, Uranus, Neptune, the Pale Blue Dot, the termination shock, Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 crossing the heliopause, the failing memory of 2023, where they are today and the long goodbye. 3 people cards (Stone, Sagan, Morabito).

- `story.json`: the story data. It is on the `cosmic` canvas and uses the new `solar-system` sky basemap. The map is a diagram of distance, not a picture of space: left to right is distance from the Sun on a logarithmic scale, and up and down show only that Voyager 1 left the planets' plane to the north and Voyager 2 to the south.
- Art added for this story: the `gas-giant`, `ringed-planet` and `ice-giant` glyphs and the `probe` traveller icon.
- Source: History by default, the way the mission was run and what it found; the cards on the flybys, the termination shock, the heliopause and the future are **Science**.
- Sources and what is uncertain: figures follow NASA and the Jet Propulsion Laboratory's Voyager mission pages. Distances today are for November 2026 and grow by about 3.6 AU a year for Voyager 1 and 3.2 AU for Voyager 2. The Oort cloud has never been seen; its existence and inner edge (perhaps 2,000 AU) are inferred from the orbits of long-period comets, and the card says so. The dates for the probes falling silent and for the stellar flybys 40,000 years from now are NASA's estimates. The termination shock and heliopause move with the solar cycle, so the distances given are those at which each probe crossed. The map adds a new sky basemap, `solar-system`, drawn on a logarithmic distance scale, and new art: the `gas-giant`, `ringed-planet` and `ice-giant` glyphs and the `probe` traveller icon.
