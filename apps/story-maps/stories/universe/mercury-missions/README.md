# Missions to Mercury

Status: built. The missions to Mercury in 11 chapters: Mariner 10’s swing past Venus and three passes, the radar hunt for ice, MESSENGER’s six and a half years to orbit, the Caloris basin, confirmed ice, a shrinking planet, MESSENGER’s last fall, BepiColombo’s launch, six flybys and a fault, and its planned arrival in orbit. 2 people cards (Colombo, Solomon).

- `story.json`: the story data. It is on the `cosmic` canvas and uses the `solar-system` sky basemap. The map is a diagram of distance, not a picture of space: left to right is distance from the Sun on a logarithmic scale, and up and down mean nothing. Mercury would fall behind the Sun on that scale, so it alone is drawn off scale, just outside the Sun’s glow, and the `note` says so. Routes show one chapter at a time (`routes: "chapter"`), and “Whole map” frames the inner Solar System (`overview`).
- Art: no new drawings; it uses the `rocky-planet` glyph added with Asteroids and Comets.
- Source: History by default; the cards on the radar echoes, MESSENGER’s ice and its findings about the planet are **Science**.
- Sources and what is uncertain: dates, distances and figures follow NASA (including the National Space Science Data Center and the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory MESSENGER pages), ESA and JAXA. Mercury is drawn off the map's logarithmic scale, as the note says. What is uncertain: the amount of polar ice (100 billion to 1 trillion tonnes) is the MESSENGER team's estimate and where it came from is argued; the size of MESSENGER's impact crater is a prediction, as the impact was not seen; the cause of the hollows and of Mercury's large core are open questions. BepiColombo's arrival in orbit in November 2026 and its science phase from 2027 are ESA's planned dates at the time of writing, so chapter 11 is written as a plan; update it once the orbit is confirmed.
