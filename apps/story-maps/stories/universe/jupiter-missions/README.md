# Missions to Jupiter

Status: built. The missions to Jupiter in 14 chapters: Pioneer 10 through the radiation belts, Pioneer 11’s closer dive, Voyager and the volcanoes of Io, Comet Shoemaker–Levy 9, Galileo’s probe, Ganymede’s magnetic field, Europa’s ocean, Cassini’s pass, Galileo’s last plunge, Juno over the poles, Inside Jupiter, Juno at the moons, JUICE and Europa Clipper. 2 people cards (Morabito, Kivelson).

- `story.json`: the story data. It is on the `cosmic` canvas with the NEW `jupiter-system` sky basemap: Jupiter to scale with its bands and Great Red Spot, the rings, Amalthea, the four Galilean moons (drawn larger than scale), the Io torus, the Himalia group and outer moons, and the edge of the magnetosphere, on a logarithmic distance scale from the cloud tops. Routes show one chapter at a time (`routes: "chapter"`).
- Art: no new drawings.
- Source: History by default; chapters 4, 6, 7, 10 and 11 are **Science**.
- Sources and what is uncertain: Dates, distances and figures follow NASA (the National Space Science Data Center, JPL and mission pages) and ESA. NASA sources give Pioneer 11’s closest approach as 34,000 or about 43,000 kilometres above the clouds, so the card says about 40,000. Morabito’s discovery is dated 9 or 11 March 1979 in different accounts, so the card says “days later”. Europa’s and Ganymede’s oceans are inferred from magnetic and other measurements, not seen; the cause of Jupiter’s dilute core is theory. Juno’s extended mission was planned to end in September 2025; whether it continued has not been confirmed here, so chapter 12 says only what was planned: update it once confirmed. Europa Clipper’s December 2026 Earth flyby, both missions’ arrivals and JUICE’s Ganymede orbit are plans at the time of writing. Ulysses also passed Jupiter, in 1992 and 2004, and is not covered.
