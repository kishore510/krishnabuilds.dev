# Missions to Venus

Status: built. The missions to Venus in 18 chapters: Mariner 2's flyby, Venera 4's fall through the air, Venera 7's landing, Venera 8 in daylight, Venera 9's first pictures, Pioneer Venus's four probes, the highest mountains, Venera 13's colour, drill and sound, the Venera 15 and 16 radar maps, the Vega balloons, Magellan's map, too few craters, Magellan's final plunge, Venus Express and the fresh lava, Akatsuki's second chance, the disputed phosphine, the volcano on Maat Mons caught changing, and the missions still to come. 4 people cards (Sagan, Babakin, Barsukov, Sagdeev).

- `story.json`: the story data. It is on the `cosmic` canvas and uses the `venus` sky basemap, whose units are longitude (east positive) and latitude, so every place is a real [lon, lat] on Venus, except the `orbit` pin, a symbolic place above the north edge for orbiters. There are no routes: every chapter has `legs: []`.
- Art added for this story: none.
- Source: History by default, the way the missions were run and what they found; chapters 7, 12, 14, 16 and 17 (the highest mountains, too few craters, fresh lava, phosphine and the changing vent on Maat Mons) are **Science**.
- Sources and what is uncertain: Landing sites, dates and durations follow NASA's National Space Science Data Center, JPL, ESA and JAXA; feature names follow the IAU Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature. Venera 4's position and the altitude where its signal stopped (about 25 km) are reconstructions, and early Soviet claims that it reached the surface were withdrawn. Venera 7's signal is given as about 23 minutes. The surface age (about 300 to 800 million years) and whether resurfacing was sudden or gradual are disputed. The 2020 phosphine detection is disputed and unconfirmed. The young lava flows at Idunn Mons (2010) and the changing vent on Maat Mons (2023) are interpretations of remote data. Chapter 18 gives plans as of November 2026 (EnVision about 2031; DAVINCI and VERITAS in the early 2030s, with their funding in doubt; India's Venus Orbiter Mission); they will change. The map is the new `venus` basemap.
