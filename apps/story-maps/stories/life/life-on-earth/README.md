# Life on Earth

Status: built. The history of life in 17 chapters: the first life, microbes make oxygen, cells with a nucleus, bodies of many cells, the Ediacaran creatures, the Cambrian explosion, onto the land, the coal forests, the Great Dying, the first dinosaurs, feathers and flight, the first flowers, after the asteroid, whales return to the sea, our ancestors stand up, our own species, and how the story was found. 3 people cards (Margulis, Walcott, Darwin).

- `story.json`: the story data. It is the second story on the `earth-deep` canvas and uses the `earth-deep` basemap, which gains three data eras for it (375, 300 and 125 million years ago).
- Art added for this story: the `trilobite`, `fish` and `dinosaur` glyphs.
- Source: every card is **Science**, except the last, which is **History**. Each card's last line says how we know.
- Sources and what is uncertain: the dates of the first life, of complex cells and of the split between our lineage and chimpanzees' are estimates, and older claims of life are disputed. How life began is unknown. Extinction percentages are estimates. Fossil sites are reconstructed with the PaleoMAP plate model (Kocsis and Scotese, CC BY 4.0); where a chapter falls between the map's eras the card says which moment the map shows. Eras before 535 million years ago are drawn by hand and are illustrative.
