The **Household budget** page shows where your money goes each month, account by account, and what is left. It is an illustration of what you enter, not advice. It **uses no tax rules**, so it has no tax year and shows "Uses no tax rules" where other pages show when their tax figures were last verified.

## What you enter

- **Your pay** is always in view: enter it **after tax**, as it reaches your main account, a month or a year. The [Take-home pay](help:take-home-pay) page can give you a figure to enter.
- **Income extras**: any other money coming in, after any tax, each with its own period.
- **Your money** holds every outgoing line. It has two views:
  - **What it's for**: a group for each kind of cost (Home, Bills, Food, Transport, Personal spending, Debt, Saving and investing, Other). Tap a line to change its name, amount, how often, group, the account that pays it and whether it is **essential**. **Add a line** adds a blank one; the buttons beside it add a common one by name. A removed line comes back with **Undo**.
  - **Where it's paid from**: your accounts. Pay lands in your **main account**. Add a **spending account** (one you top up for day-to-day spending), a **joint account** (enter your own share of what goes in and what it pays for) or a **savings pot**, each with the amount you move into it from main. **Start from** sets up a common arrangement in one go. Rename an account or change its kind in place; **Delete** moves its lines back to main, with **Undo**.

The page starts with named lines (Rent or mortgage, Council tax, Energy, Phone and broadband, Groceries, Eating out, Fuel or travel, Car insurance, Shopping, Credit card payment, Cash savings, Stocks and shares ISA) as a template. A blank amount counts as nothing.

Each line and transfer is paid **weekly**, **monthly**, **quarterly** or **yearly**. A week counts as the weeks in a year and a quarter as the quarters, so a year is exact.

Moving money between your own accounts is not spending: each cost is counted once, from the account that pays it. Enter a card payment or the spending on that card, **not both**, or it is counted twice.

## The results

- **Left over across your accounts**, with its share **of your income**, is always in view: on a phone in the bar at the top, with any account that runs short named under it.
- **Left in each account**: main after its bills, saving and transfers out; a spending or joint account's transfer less what it pays; what a savings pot puts aside (counted as saved, not as left over).
- An account that pays out more than goes in **runs short**: the page says by how much, that you're topping it up from main, and offers **Move £… a month** to set its transfer to cover it (with **Undo**). Main's card shows what is really left after the top-up.
- **Take-home**, **Going out** and the **saving rate**: the saving group and what savings pots put aside, as a share of your income.
- **Where your income goes**: a ring split by group, savings pots and what is left, with a table.
- **Your biggest outgoings**: the three largest lines outside saving.
- **50/30/20 check**: your needs (essential lines), wants (the rest) and saving as shares of your income, beside that common rule of thumb. It is a comparison, not advice.
- **By group**: each group's amount and share of your income.
- **Month** or **Year** switches every figure. A month is the year divided by the months, to the nearest penny, so month rows can differ from their total by a penny, and the page says so.

With no income, shares show as "n/a". When more goes out than comes in, what is left is shown below nothing, with a warning, never hidden.

## What if

In **What if**, a slider and a box for your pay, each transfer and each line let you try other amounts. The results then show your inputs and the what-ifs side by side, with the change. What-ifs are **never saved**: **Reset what-ifs** drops them, and **Apply to my inputs** puts them into your inputs (with **Undo**).

## Share

**Share** makes a copy of your budget to send to someone, on this device; nothing is uploaded. Choose:

- **Image**: a card for messages and social posts. Download it, or on a phone share it straight to an app.
- **PDF**: an A4 summary. In the print window, choose **Save as PDF**.
- **Spreadsheet**: every line, a month and a year, and each account, as a CSV file for Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.
- **Text**: a short summary to copy and paste.

Turn off **Show amounts** to share every figure as a share of your income instead, and **Include line names** to share groups only.

## Budgets saved before 0.33.0

A budget saved before accounts existed opens with the same left over: spending lines go into a group by their name (or Other), and each joint account line becomes a joint account with one line, "Spending from this account", for what you moved into it. Split that line into what the joint account really pays for whenever you like.

## Not modelled

Tax, National Insurance, ISA and pension rules; interest, growth and inflation; how your budget changes over time; payslip timing (four-weekly or weekly pay months); importing from the take-home calculator. These are not modelled.
