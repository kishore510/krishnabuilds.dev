Krishnabuilds Arcade

Changelog

What's new and what got fixed, roughly grouped by session — not every commit, just the stuff worth knowing about.

Nov 5, 2026

  • FIXDojo Duel — on short phone screens the krishnabuilds menu at the bottom covered the Start button, and the start screen wouldn't scroll far enough to get past it. The start and result screens now keep their content clear of the bottom buttons and scroll properly, Start sits right under the opponent picker (the controls list moved below it), and the mute and hitbox buttons only show during a fight instead of sitting on top of the start screen text.
  • NEWDojo Duel step 6 — energy and power-ups. A blue energy meter sits under each health bar: it starts each round about a third full, refills slowly on its own and a little faster when you land hits, and stops refilling while you block. Spend it on three power-ups: 1 throws a Fireball (it can be blocked, and a clean hit pops a POW!), 2 is a Speed boost (faster walking and quicker recovery for a few seconds) and 3 raises a Shield that soaks up one hit. On touch, tap one of the three icons next to the Power button to pick it, then press Power; icons fade when you can't afford them. Try one without enough energy and the meter flashes "NO ENERGY" with a dull click. REN uses them too, under the same rules: rarely on Easy, and on Hard it throws fireballs from range, boosts in to press and shields up when it's hurt.
  • FIXDojo Duel — attacks that looked like they should land now do. Holding back to block used to also walk you backwards, so REN often stepped out of reach mid-kick and nothing happened at all; now holding back against an incoming attack plants you in a guard (for you and REN alike), so you see the block. A punch that meets REN's raised fist now counts as a hit, and on Easy REN stands within reach more often instead of hovering just outside it.
  • NEWDojo Duel step 5 — REN fights back. Pick Easy or Hard on the start screen (Easy is the default): REN walks in, pokes with punches, kicks and sweeps, blocks what it sees coming, punishes a missed attack, jabs you out of a jump-in and backs off after taking a hit. Easy reacts slowly and often forgets to block; Hard is quick and much harder to open up. REN plays fair: it only uses your moves and has to see what you did before it can react. The old training dummy lives on as Practice mode, and the result screen has a Change opponent button.
  • NEWDojo Duel step 4 — rounds. Matches are now best of 3, with a 60-second clock at the top, a "Round 1 / Fight!" banner to start each round, round win markers under the health bars, and a "You win" / "You lose" screen with Play Again. Knock your opponent out for a K.O. and a giant WHAM!; if time runs out, whoever has more health takes the round, and a tie replays it. Everyday punches and kicks no longer pop a POW! or WHAM! (the hit still flashes, freezes, sparks and thuds); comic bursts are saved for the knockout blow and future power moves.
  • NEWDojo Duel step 3 — hits feel like hits. A landed punch or kick freezes the action for a split second (longer for kicks), the fighter who's hit flashes white and reels back, and a comic POW! or WHAM! bursts out of the impact with a shower of sparks. Kicks shake the screen. New sounds for punches, kicks, blocks and missed swings, all silenced by the mute button. Blocked hits just spark, thud and push the blocker back.
  • FIXDojo Duel — arrow keys now work on keyboards and remote desktops that report them in an unusual way (seen on a Windows work laptop, where J worked but the arrows did nothing). The hitbox view also shows the last key pressed, to help track down any key that still doesn't respond.
  • NEWDojo Duel step 2 — punches and kicks (standing, crouching and in the air), blocking with chip damage (crouch to block sweeps, stand to block air attacks), working health bars, and a training dummy you can set to stand, block or low block. Both fighters are bigger, and the hitbox view now shows each move's name and frame count.
  • NEWDojo Duel (work in progress) — a landscape one-on-one fighting game. This first update is movement only: walk, jump and crouch in the dojo, with keyboard or touch controls and a hitbox view (H or the Boxes button). Punches, kicks, rounds and a CPU that fights back come next.
