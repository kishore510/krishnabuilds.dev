HOW TO PLAY

LEFT THUMB · RUN
Drag anywhere on the left to run. Get beside the ball; let go and your feet settle the last step.

RIGHT THUMB · SWIPE UP
Topspin. Swipe as the ring closes on the ball. The swipe's direction aims; a longer swipe goes deeper.

FAST FLICK · FLAT DRIVE
Harder and lower, but it sprays if your timing is off.

SLOW SWIPE · LOB
High and deep, over a player at the net.

SWIPE DOWN · DROP SHOT
Soft, with backspin, just over the net.

CURVED SWIPE · CURVEBALL
Bend the swipe and the ball bends in the air, then kicks sideways.

COME TO THE NET
Run forward behind a deep shot. At the net any swipe volleys, and a high ball is a smash.

SERVE
Touch and hold on the right to toss, then flick up as the ball reaches the top. Fast is flat, gentler is a kick.
