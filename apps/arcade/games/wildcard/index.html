      How to play

      1. Play a card that matches the colour or the number or symbol on top of the pile.
      2. Wild cards go on anything. You pick the next colour.
      3. Nothing to play? Tap the deck to draw. If the new card fits, play it or keep it.
      4. Skip (⊘) jumps the next player. Reverse (⇄) turns play around (against one bot it works like a Skip). +2 and +4 make the next player draw and miss a go.
      5. Down to two cards? Tap Last card! before you play, or draw two.
      6. First to empty their hand wins.
