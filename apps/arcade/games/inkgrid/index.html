How to play

Numbers along the top and side tell you the blocks of filled squares in that row or column, in order. Each number is one unbroken block, with at least one empty square before the next — e.g. "1 2" means a single filled square, a gap, then two filled squares in a row.

Tap Fill to paint a square, Mark to note a square you're sure is empty — marking helps you keep track while you work out the rest.

When a row or column is fully solved, its clue dims and gets crossed out.
