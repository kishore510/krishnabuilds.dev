LEVEL 1 · MOSSY FORD

Forest Explorer

Hop the stepping stones, climb the vines, find the three hidden chests and pull the old lever to reach the forest gate.

Easy: five hearts and sleepy animals. Hard: three hearts, and the fox and bee come after you.

Phone: left thumb to walk, drag on the right to look, JUMP and ACT buttons · Keyboard: WASD / arrows, Space to jump, E to act, mouse drag or Q / R to look, P to pause

Best played in landscape.

Forest, rocks and textures: Poly Haven scans (CC0), trees and elk by Igor_K., LadyLionStudios and WildMesh_3D on Sketchfab (CC BY), hero and animals by Quaternius (CC0). Full credits in the game folder.
